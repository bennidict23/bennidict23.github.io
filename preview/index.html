Chubin Zhang

I am currently a Research Assistant at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), fortunate to be advised by Prof. Bo An. Previously, I worked as a Research Intern at A*STAR Centre for Frontier AI Research (CFAR) advised by Prof. Ivor Tsang, and at Tsinghua University (THUNLP) advised by Prof. Zhiyuan Liu. I received my B.Eng. degree with First Class Honours (Top 12%) from BUPT in 2026.

My research focuses on building principled, expressive, and controllable decision-making systems. Specifically, my work spans two closely connected threads:

  • Generative & Online Reinforcement Learning: Decoupling policy optimization from expressive diffusion/flow generation for continuous control & robot learning (e.g., GoRL, ICML 2026).
  • Reliable & Controllable AI Agents: Intervention advantage, calibration, and evidence-grounded stopping decisions in tool-using LLM agents (e.g., NeurIPS 2026).

Calibration Is Not Control: Intervention Value for LLM-Agent Oversight

Chubin Zhang, Zhenglin Wan, Xingrui Yu, Jingxuan Wu, Qi Wen, Pengfei Zhou, Wangbo Zhao, Ivor Tsang

TL;DR: Demonstrates why standard uncertainty calibration alone fails to guarantee effective oversight in autonomous LLM agents, and introduces Intervention Advantage to quantify and optimize human/verifier control utility.

ICML 2026
ICML 2026 ★ First Author

Generative Online Reinforcement Learning (GoRL)

Chubin Zhang, Zhenglin Wan, Feng Chen, Fuchao Yang, Lang Feng, Yaxin Zhou, Xingrui Yu, Yang You, Ivor Tsang, Bo An

TL;DR: Decouples policy optimization from multimodal action generation via a tractable latent policy and diffusion/flow decoders with two-timescale training—stabilizing online RL without backpropagating through long sampling chains and achieving >3× return over the strongest baseline on HopperStand.

arXiv Preprint 2026 ★ First Author

Judged Useless, Queried Anyway: Tool-Using Agents Rarely Turn Their Own Evidence Judgments into Stopping Decisions

Chubin Zhang, Zhenglin Wan, Xingrui Yu, Jingxuan Wu, Yaxin Zhou, Ivor Tsang, Bo An

TL;DR: Uncovers a critical belief-action gap in tool-using LLM agents: even when agents explicitly judge retrieved evidence as unhelpful, they rarely translate that judgment into early stopping decisions.
