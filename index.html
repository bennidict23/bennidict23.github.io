😊 About Me

I am a Research Assistant at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), working with Prof. Bo An. Before that, I was a research intern with Prof. Ivor Tsang at A*STAR CFAR and with Prof. Zhiyuan Liu at THUNLP, Tsinghua University. I received my B.Eng. from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT).

My research interests are in reinforcement learning and embodied AI. Recently, I have worked on training generative policies with online RL and on decision-making in LLM agents.

I am looking for PhD positions starting in 2027 and am also open to research internships. Feel free to reach out by email!

Generative Online Reinforcement Learning

Chubin Zhang, Zhenglin Wan, Feng Chen, Fuchao Yang, Lang Feng, Yaxin Zhou, Xingrui Yu, Yang You, Ivor Tsang, Bo An

International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), 2026

Makes diffusion and flow policies trainable with online RL by separating latent policy optimization from action generation. Over 3x the return of the strongest baseline on HopperStand.

NeurIPS 2026

Calibration Is Not Control: Intervention Value for LLM-Agent Oversight

Chubin Zhang, Zhenglin Wan, Xingrui Yu, Jingxuan Wu, Qi Wen, Pengfei Zhou, Wangbo Zhao, Ivor Tsang

Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), 2026

Predicting that an agent will fail does not tell an overseer whether stepping in will help. We decide when to intervene by comparing the value of intervening with the value of letting the agent continue.

Paper Code
Preprint

Judged Useless, Queried Anyway: Tool-Using Agents Rarely Turn Their Own Evidence Judgments into Stopping Decisions

Chubin Zhang, Zhenglin Wan, Xingrui Yu, Jingxuan Wu, Yaxin Zhou, Ivor Tsang, Bo An

arXiv preprint, 2026

LLM agents judge a failing tool's results useless 97–100% of the time, yet keep querying it. Forcing an answer after five such judgments raises success for every model tested.
