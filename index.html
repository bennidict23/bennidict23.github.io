Selected work

From online policy learning to reliable agent oversight.

NeurIPS 2026First author

Calibration Is Not Control

Knowing that an agent might fail does not tell us whether intervening will help. We study intervention advantage through action-conditioned decisions and counterfactual branching from the same trajectory prefix.

Preprint ↗ arXiv version uses an earlier title
ICML 2026First author

Generative Online Reinforcement Learning

GoRL separates tractable latent policy optimization from flow and diffusion action generation, making expressive policies easier to train through online interaction.
